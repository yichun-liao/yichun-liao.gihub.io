Technical Expertise & Research Focus

My primary technical strengths and research domains encompass the following specialized areas:

  • System Identification & Control: Formulating mathematical system models from empirical input-output data, and applying optimal control algorithms to control the system.
  • State Estimation & Sensor Fusion: Deriving quaternion-based system models and update matrices (Jacobians) to implement Extended Kalman Filters (EKF) for accurate IMU orientation tracking.
  • Control Theory & Dynamic Simulation: Developing robust feedback control loops, and simulated physical systems with MATLAB Simulink & Simscape.
  • Quantitative Value Investing Strategies: Designing systematic, rule-based algorithmic screening models based on fundamental financial metrics and multi-stage systematic profit-taking logic.
  • Backtesting & Financial Data Analytics: Constructing rigorous testing environments leveraging databases to validate algorithmic robust performance across historical stress scenarios.

Technical Approach

"Data provides the model, but analytical structure provides the safety guarantees."

Real-world dynamic systems rarely match textbook assumptions— sensor data carries noise, system dynamics shift, and financial time-series exhibit heavy tail risks.

My approach focuses on bridging statistical learning with formal dynamic guarantees: deriving analytical update matrices for state estimation, constructing data-driven Control Barrier Functions (CBFs) for safety, and stress-testing algorithms against edge cases before implementation.
