01 // Executive Biography

Welcome to my digital workspace. I am an Electrical, Computer & Energy Engineering (ECE) Ph.D. student at the University of Colorado Boulder, specializing in data-driven safety-critical control and statistical system identification. My doctoral research centers on synthesizing Control Barrier Functions (CBFs) and optimization-based controller safety filters directly from empirical trajectory data to provide formal safety guarantees for uncertain dynamical systems—bridging statistical modeling, optimal control, and closed-loop physical simulation.

TECHNICAL_APPROACH.LOG // CORE THESIS
"Data provides the model, but analytical structure provides the safety guarantees."

Real-world physical systems rarely match idealized textbook assumptions—measurements carry noise, and dynamics often involve unmodeled uncertainties or external disturbances. My approach bridges statistical learning with formal control theory: characterizing uncertainty bounds from finite data, constructing data-driven Control Barrier Functions (CBFs) for invariance, and stress-testing controllers against edge cases in simulation before deployment.
